Do not upload a full .claude folder.

Agent folders can contain tokens, private prompts, shell-like logs, internal paths, customer data, tool outputs, or business secrets.

  • A safer project or agent-system import will be a separate HAI Context Bundle later.
  • That bundle needs allowlists, secret scanning, redaction, preview, manifest, consent, and deletion rules.
