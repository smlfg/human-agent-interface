02 / The Rule

Ownership

One domain, one job.

The heart explains. Sales sells. Tools prove. No domain repeats another — otherwise two pages compete for the same search and the same claim.

The canonical address always points to the domain that owns the content. Not to the one a visitor happened to arrive through.

This is not an SEO exercise. It is the same rule as in operations: one job, one owner, one source.

Litmus test: can the job be stated in one sentence? If not, two things belong on two domains.

03 / Live

Inventory

One address is bound.

www.human-agent-interface.com is the only own domain serving these pages today. It is the heart and the canonical source.

agentfähig.de is intended as the sales address and named in the imprint and privacy policy. There is no route for agentfähig.de in the worker.

If the domain simply pointed at the same worker, the asset layer would serve the homepage — with the homepage’s canonical. Two hosts, one page, and the sales domain would introduce itself as the heart. That is duplicate content with the wrong owner.

The planned fix is a host rewrite: requests to agentfähig.de are rewritten to /agentfaehig/. The sales page already exists there; its canonical currently points to the heart. After the rewrite, the canonical must point to the owning domain.

Until both are checked, agentfähig.de stays unbound.

Verified 30 Oct 2026: the sales page at /agentfaehig/ is live; the domain agentfähig.de is not bound to this worker. The absent route and the sales page’s canonical are verified. Not verified: the DNS state of agentfähig.de.

04 / Next Steps

Sequence

Four steps, checked one by one.

No step starts before the previous one can be checked.

  1. 01

    Rewrite agentfähig.de and fix its canonical.

    Host rewrite to /agentfaehig/, canonical to the sales domain, test on a preview. Only then bind.

  2. 02

    Ship sales separately.

    Its own deploy for the sales page. The heart is not rolled out together with sales.

  3. 03

    Bind the remaining domains one at a time.

    One domain per step, each with its own route, canonical and content. No bundle.

  4. 04

    Keep one deploy source.

    03_HAI only. Check commit and date before every deploy.

Abort criterion: two domains with the same content are not progress, but a regression.

05 / Checkouts

Operational risk

Three checkouts, one risk.

The same repository exists in three places. Only one is the deploy state.

A deploy from the wrong folder publishes an old state — without an error, without a warning. The site looks intact and is still wrong.
