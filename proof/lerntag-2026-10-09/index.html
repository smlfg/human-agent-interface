12 / HAI

What HAI means here

Intention, execution, evidence, decision, updated state

The human steered through intentions and decisions; agents executed across system boundaries.

The goal was not automated productivity but infrastructure in which the learner's own ability can grow. The day's clearest finding came from the human: help on request worked, the proactive tutor did not.

Still missing: reliable feedback. When progress is confirmed in Notion, tutor and calendar should take that state into account automatically.
