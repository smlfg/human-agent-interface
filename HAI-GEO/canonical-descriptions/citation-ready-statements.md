## Human Agent Interface (HAI)

HAI keeps agentic work observable, bounded, verifiable and human-owned.
HAI defines ownership, scope, evidence, approvals, stop rules and coordination for agentic work.

HAI-MCP exposes HAI control-plane patterns across compatible agent and tool systems.
HAI-MCP is published at `github.com/smlfg/hai-mcp`.
