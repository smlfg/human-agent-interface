# Can I Really Do This? HAI Live Harness Reality Check

Checked: 2026-05-18

Question:

```text
Kann Samuel das wirklich?
```

Short verdict:

```text
Yes, as a bounded HAI Harness Operator / Onboarding practice.
No, not yet as a big "School of Agents", certification platform, or scalable paid voice product.
```

The credible version is not:

```text
I am the master who never makes agent mistakes.
```

The credible version is:

```text
I have made many of the typical agent mistakes, and HAI is the method for making those mistakes smaller, earlier, and less dangerous.
```

There is already more than a loose idea.

Existing project evidence:

- `HAI_MUENZTELEFON_V0.md` captures the original voice/payment/session concept and separates V0 from V1.
- `CLAUDE_CODE_AGENT_CONTEXT_PACKET.md` defines a real Claude Code onboarding scope, sources, risks, and level 1/2/3 teaching path.
- `CODEX_AGENT_CONTEXT_PACKET.md` does the same for Codex.
- `HERMES_AGENT_CONTEXT_PACKET.md` covers the Hermes side.
- `LIVE_AGENT_SHELL_CONTEXT_PACKET.md` covers routing, session shell, payment/voice prior art, consent, and state.
- `FREE_SCHOOL_OF_AGENTS_PRIOR_ART.md` shows the free web is already strong for building agents, but weak at the human operator layer.
- `index.html` and `app.js` already contain a text-only V0 shell with harness selection, level selection, constraints, routing label, timer, message cap, summary area, consent checkbox, and contact CTA.

This means the thing exists as:

```text
concept -> product spec -> prior-art context -> harness packets -> small demo shell
```

It is not just fantasy.

## 2. What Samuel Can Credibly Do

Samuel can credibly do this narrow job:

```text
Help a person turn a messy agent/harness situation into a bounded, safer next step.
```

Why:

- He has lived the actual failure modes: too much output, too many agents, scope drift, unclear ownership, weak verification.
- He already thinks in the right axes: human ownership, agent permissions, session limits, evidence, stop rules, cognitive load.
- The HAI product is not pretending to out-teach Hugging Face, OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Google, or LangChain.
- The gap is translation: official docs and free courses -> human-owned operator workflow.
- The local V0 already avoids the dangerous promise of "I will do your work"; it frames itself as onboarding, routing, and summary.

The honest sell is not expertise-as-perfection.

The honest sell is:

```text
I know how agent work goes wrong, and I can help you notice the wrongness before it becomes expensive.
```

The current truth is:

```text
You have a promising operator-method and a small demo. You do not yet have market proof.
```

## 4. The Central Personal Risk

The risk is not that Samuel makes mistakes.

The risk is pretending that mistakes disqualify him, or trying to compensate by building a huge system.

The mistakes are actually the source of the product if they are processed correctly:

- "I overbuilt" becomes "V0 must stay bounded."
- "I launched too many agents" becomes "no uncontrolled fanout."
- "I lost the thread" becomes "session summary and one next step."
- "I trusted outputs too quickly" becomes "evidence before done."
- "I got cognitively overloaded" becomes "operator layer, not dashboard."

The product is credible only if it stays close to this lived evidence.

## 5. The Smallest Real Proof

Do not prove this by building more infrastructure.

Prove it with 5 real sessions.

Each session can be text-only and manual. Payment and voice are not required.

Session protocol:

1. Person chooses a harness: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, OpenCode, or unknown.
2. Person describes one recent agent problem.
3. Samuel or the demo asks only enough to classify:
   - Orientierung
   - Setup / Konfiguration
   - Workflow / HAI-Steuerung
4. End after 10 minutes or 8-12 messages.
5. Produce a session packet:
   - current problem
   - selected harness
   - what the agent must not do
   - one next safe step
   - one verification rule
   - optional contact/follow-up

Success condition:

```text
After the session, the person can say what they should do next and what they should not let the agent do.
```

If 3 out of 5 sessions achieve that, the V0 is real.

If not, the concept should be sharpened before building voice, payment, or a course.

## 6. What To Build Next

Do not build "School of Agents" next.

Do not build payment next.

Do not build voice next.

Build the proof loop:

```text
5 manual HAI Harness Checks
1 saved session packet per check
1 note: what confused the user?
1 note: what helped?
1 decision: does this deserve V1?
```

The next concrete artifact should be:

```text
MANUAL_HARNESS_CHECK_PROTOCOL_V0.md
```

That protocol should be used before any more platform work.

## 7. Final Verdict

Samuel can do the narrow, useful version.

He cannot yet honestly claim the broad version.

The strong path is:

```text
Do not become a teacher of "agents".
Become the person who helps humans stay owners while using agent harnesses.
```

That is specific enough to be useful, honest enough to be credible, and small enough to test.
