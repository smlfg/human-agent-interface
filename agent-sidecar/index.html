Agent sidecar: a handoff checklist and worked review

An agent sidecar can prepare a handoff for review by gathering what was requested, what happened and what still needs a decision. While observation surfaces signals, the handoff gives the next person context to inspect and act on.

Checklist

  1. Goal and boundary: What outcome was requested, and what was excluded?
  2. Work done: Which concrete artifacts changed? Separate proposals from completed actions.
  3. Evidence: Which checks were actually performed, with what results? Name anything not checked.
  4. Open decisions: What exceeds the agreement, who can decide, and what happens while it is open?
  5. Review: Can the reviewer inspect the relevant change and either accept it or request a correction?

Worked review

Illustrative packet: The agent sidecar gathers the reported work and checks, shows the signup-form boundary, and marks the policy suggestion as an open decision for the reviewer. Goal: clarify the empty-email error. Boundary: signup form only. Work reported: changed the error text in the form. Evidence: the agent reports checking an empty field and a filled field; the reviewer has not yet repeated those checks. Proposal: revise account policy too. Decision: human review required before adding that work.

Review outcome: The person inspects the form change and repeats the two checks. If both behave as described and the message is clear, they can accept the form change. The policy suggestion remains a separate proposal, not an accepted extension. If either check fails, the reviewer returns the specific result for correction.
