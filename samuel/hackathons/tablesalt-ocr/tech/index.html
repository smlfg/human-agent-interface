Perspective 01 — Technical

The technical chronology

What was built, measured, kept and thrown away — in the order it happened, with the numbers that decided each decision.

Evidence vocabulary

Every number on this site carries one of these six labels. They are not decoration — they say how much the number is allowed to weigh.

  • official

    Produced by the organiser’s own scorer on the organiser’s own data. These are the numbers we treat as ground truth.

  • local

    Reproduced by us on data we generated ourselves. Directionally useful, but not directly comparable to official numbers.

  • documented

    Stated verbatim in a project document — a kick-off note, a plan, an ADR. A statement of intent or a decision, not a measurement.

  • reported

    Reported by an agent or a parallel session and quoted as-is. We have not re-verified it inside this repository.

  • hypothesis

    A proposal that was written down before it was measured. No measurement behind it yet.

  • inferred

    A conclusion we drew from the evidence. The source says something close, but not exactly this.

  2. Structure model 20 Nov 2026 Exact time of day not recorded. official Evidence: Official scorer, official data

    Tesseract floor, then TATR recovers from a 0.08 fax collapse with two lines outside the model

    The classical floor is measured first: Tesseract 5.5.3 on dev scores FINAL 0.3417 — below the organizer’s published 0.348, later traced to a double JPEG re-encode before pytesseract. Table Transformer (TATR, 28.8M params, DETR ResNet-18) is then evaluated: as-is structure scores weighted TEDS 0.589 because T3 (fax) collapses to 0.083 from duplicate row/column boxes on speckle. Adding a 3×3 median filter and greedy NMS (IoU 0.3) lifts T3 structure to 0.946 (weighted TEDS 0.9365 with oracle text, 23/24 shapes exact), while entropy deskew costs 0.21 TEDS on T2 and is dropped. Paired with per-cell Tesseract, TATR reaches FINAL 0.6608 on dev (T0 0.831, T1 0.877, T2 0.738, T3 0.623, QA 0.275, weighted TEDS 0.7289).

    Technical reading

    The defect in T3 was duplicate detections on sensor noise, not capacity, resolution, or skew — fixed outside the weights with a pre-filter and post-filter. Yet even patched, TATR + Tesseract leaves a massive gap between T3 structure (0.946) and T3 end-to-end (0.623): text, not structure, turns out to be the binding constraint.

  2. End-to-end VLM (G2) 20 Nov 2026 Exact time of day not recorded. breakthrough official Evidence: Official scorer, official data

    MinerU2.5 1.2B clears the structure ceiling in one pass

    A 1.2B-parameter vision-language model (bf16), run over MLX at native resolution (`max_edge=0`, prompt "Table Recognition:"), emits OTSL tokens converted to cells by `pipeline/convert.py`. It scores FINAL 0.9659 on dev (T0 0.959, T1 0.992, T2 0.956, T3 0.966, QA 0.958, CellF1 0.9369) at 2.56 s/table and 0.9621 on val96 (seed 2: T0 0.986, T1 0.975, T2 0.945, T3 0.963, QA 0.960) at 2.33 s/table — overtaking the hand-built classical pipeline.

    Technical reading

    The architecture collapsed from "structure model + OCR model + assembler" to "one model + converter" (G2). Crucially, "raw" 0.9659 already depends on our OTSL-to-cell footprinting converter.

  1. Post-processing (G3) 20 Nov 2026 Exact time of day not recorded. breakthrough official Evidence: Official scorer, official data

    P0–P5: six post-processing rules buy the last two points

    Six deterministic passes are added in `pipeline/postprocess.py`: P0 `collapse_phantom_columns`, P1 `normalize_dashes`, P2 `normalize_number_format`, P3 `split_numeric_colspans` (deskewed pure-NumPy ink column projection), P4 `repair_header_corner_rowspan`, and P5 `merge_split_placeholders`. Unicode dash normalization alone is worth +0.0124 FINAL on dev (0.9659 → 0.9783) and +0.0085 on val96 (0.9621 → 0.9705). Together with 0/120 ground-truth cells harmed: dev FINAL 0.9862 (T3 1.0000, QA 0.958), val96 FINAL 0.9808 (T3 0.9891, QA 0.960) — though both synthetic sets were used adaptively to develop the rules.

    Technical reading

    The largest remaining gain is not a better model but deterministic, geometry-aware rules tied 1-to-1 to named error classes in ERRORS.md, with no hardcoded dataset vocabulary. P3 and P5 re-project ink from the original image and only act when pixel geometry proves it. Note the methodological caveat: dev and val96 were both used adaptively to build and verify these rules.
